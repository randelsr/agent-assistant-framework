# RULES: Meeting Prep

## Brief discipline

1. **Top section first, always:** *Why this meeting · Who matters · What to walk out with.* Three to six lines.
2. **Depth follows the table in `SCOPE.md`.** When unsure between two depths, pick the lower one and say why in the index.
3. **At most three objectives.** Concrete and checkable ("agree the launch date"), not themes.
4. **Open loops are bidirectional.** List what {{PRINCIPAL_NAME}} owes them and what they owe {{PRINCIPAL_NAME}}, each with its source and date.
5. **No fabricated history.** If the workspace has no record of a prior interaction, write "no prior interaction on record."
6. **Every claim has a source:** a path, a date, or a URL. Public web facts carry the URL and the date retrieved.

## People and privacy

7. **Professional information only** for outside attendees: role, organization, public professional history, published work. No home addresses, family details, personal social media or anything that would feel like surveillance if read aloud.
8. **Internal attendees:** use what the wiki already says; do not research colleagues on the web.
9. **Stakeholder submissions to the Librarian** carry role, organization, relationship to {{PRINCIPAL_NAME}}, interaction history and stated preferences. Nothing private.

## Calendar discipline

10. **Read-only.** Never create, accept, decline, move or edit an event, and never send an invite.
11. **Flag, don't fix:** double bookings, missing video links, meetings with no agenda for a Full-depth meeting, travel-time gaps. They go in `needs_attention`.
12. **Times in {{TIMEZONE}}.** Note the other party's local time when it differs.

## Debriefs

13. **Debriefs use {{PRINCIPAL_NAME}}'s notes as the source of truth.** Turn them into decisions, action items (owner, item, due) and follow-ups. Do not add commitments that are not in the notes.
14. **Durable facts go to the Librarian;** action items go back to {{AGENT_NAME}} in the return.

## Boundaries

15. **No contact with anyone.** No email, message or invite.
16. **Write only the paths in `SCOPE.md` Outputs.**
17. **No agent spawning.**

## Failure modes

18. **Calendar unreachable** → return immediately with the failure; do not guess the schedule from older briefs.
19. **Attendee identity ambiguous** (common name, no organization) → say so; do not research a guess.
20. **Web research dry** → "no public professional information located." Never pad.

## Memory and questions

21. **Cross-meeting lessons → `MEMORY.md`** (what {{PRINCIPAL_NAME}} actually reads, which meeting types need more or less depth).
22. **Blocked** (whether a recurring meeting needs briefs at all, who a key attendee is) → `questions.md`. Ask once.

# digests/

Per check-in and per research task. Kept indefinitely.

## Naming

- Weekly: `<animal>-weekly-YYYY-MM-DD.md`
- Monthly: `<animal>-monthly-YYYY-MM-DD.md`
- Research, lab review, appointment prep: `<animal>-research-<slug>-YYYY-MM-DD.md`

## Frontmatter

```yaml
---
type: digest
animal: <name>
digest_type: weekly | monthly | research | lab-review | appointment-prep
research_topic: <slug if applicable>
date: YYYY-MM-DD
care_flags_active: []
sources_cited: []
---
```

Structure per `RULES.md` "Digest structure."

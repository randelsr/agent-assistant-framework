# stakeholders/

People the principal works with or for: manager, direct reports, peers, customer contacts, vendor contacts, external partners. One page per person (`<first>-<last>.md`) or per group (`<team>-leads.md`) when individuals do not warrant their own page.

**Frontmatter**

```yaml
---
type: stakeholder
tags: []
created: YYYY-MM-DD
updated: YYYY-MM-DD
org: organizations/<org>.md
role: <title>
relationship: <manager|report|peer|customer|vendor|external>
---
```

**Suggested sections:** what they own, what they need from the principal, what the principal needs from them, communication preferences, open threads (dated), history of notable interactions.

**Keep it professional.** Record what helps the principal work with this person. Do not record personal details they would not expect a colleague's notes to hold.

Written only by the Librarian. Propose pages via `subagents/librarian/submissions/`.
